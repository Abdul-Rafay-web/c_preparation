CERN 2027 CRACK PLAN — FROM BEGINNER TO COMPETITIVE APPLICANT
Start: 14 October 2026
Target: CERN Summer Student Programme + CERN openlab Summer 2027

IMPORTANT REALITY
You are NOT starting from zero as an engineer. Your gap is CERN-specific knowledge and proof:
particle physics, scientific Python, PyTorch depth, efficient ML, FPGA-aware ML, modern C++, CERN data tooling, and research-style evidence.

Can you crack it?
YES, you can become a genuinely competitive applicant if you execute hard and consistently.
NO plan can guarantee selection. The official campaign itself says selection is never guaranteed and the goal is to become an obvious match for multiple supervisors.

The original campaign was designed around ~20 focused hours/week. Since the calendar has already moved from the original Sep 29 start, this version compresses and prioritizes rather than pretending lost weeks do not matter.

CORE STRATEGY
Do NOT try to master every CERN technology.
Build 3 strong pillars:

A. Efficient ML for particle triggers — MAIN DIFFERENTIATOR
   Project: TriggerNet
B. LLM/RAG + scientific knowledge systems — EXISTING STRENGTH UPGRADED
   Project: LUMI
C. Scientific software + CERN data + full stack — SUPPORTING PILLAR
   Project: Open Data Explorer

NON-NEGOTIABLE OUTCOME
TriggerNet Level 2 > LUMI evaluated benchmark > Open Data Explorer > 2–3 useful open-source PRs > strong application package.
